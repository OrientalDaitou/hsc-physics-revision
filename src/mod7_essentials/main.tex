\unnumberedchapter[\faInfoCircle]{How to use these Essentials}

This is the short, knowledge-only companion to the full \textbf{HSC Physics Revision Guide}
for Module~\modulenumber\ (\moduletitle). It covers every syllabus dot point (NSW Physics
Stage~6 Syllabus, 2017), with no questions. The worked examples, Quick checks and the
practice set with answers are in the full guide.

\subsection*{What the coloured boxes mean}

\begin{sidebyside}[0.5]
  \begin{bigidea}[before skip=0pt, after skip=0pt, equal height group=esslegA, fontupper=\normalsize]
    Violet: the main point of a section in one to three sentences. Read it first.
  \end{bigidea}
\nextside
  \begin{keywords}[before skip=0pt, after skip=0pt, equal height group=esslegA]
    \kw{Term}{teal: a key word and its meaning. New words are \term{bold}.}
  \end{keywords}
\end{sidebyside}

\begin{formulacard}[unbreakable, before skip=6pt, after skip=6pt]{Name of the formula}
  \cardformula{E = hf}
  Blue: the formula as on the official sheet, its symbols with SI units, when to use it
  and a common trap. The badge is about the official HSC formula sheet:
  \begin{itemize}[itemsep=0pt, topsep=1pt]
    \item \hscbadgeonsheet\ \ printed on the sheet: you are given it in the exam;
    \item \hscbadgenotonsheet\ \ learn it, or learn how to derive it;
    \item \hscbadgecombine{two formulas}\ \ join two formulas.
  \end{itemize}
\end{formulacard}

\begin{sidebyside}[0.5]
  \begin{experiment}[unbreakable, before skip=0pt, after skip=0pt, equal height group=esslegB]{}

    Navy: \emph{In this chapter} and \emph{Remember}: the must-know points.
  \end{summarybox}
\end{sidebyside}

\subsection*{How to study with these Essentials}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Read one chapter a day.} Start each section with its key idea. Learn the
        formulas with the red badge. If a part is not clear, read it in the full guide.
  \item \textbf{The night before the exam} (Wednesday 4~November 2026), re-read the
        \emph{Remember} boxes and \emph{All formulas at a glance}.
\end{itemize}
