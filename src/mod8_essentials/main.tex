\unnumberedchapter[\faInfoCircle]{How to use these Essentials}

This is the short, knowledge-only companion to the full \textbf{HSC Physics Revision Guide}
for Module~\modulenumber\ (\moduletitle). It covers every syllabus dot point (NSW Physics
Stage~6 Syllabus, 2017) as knowledge points only: there are no questions. The worked
examples, the Quick checks and the practice set with full answers are in the full guide.

\subsection*{What the coloured boxes mean}

{\renewcommand\arraystretch{1.25}\arrayrulecolor{hscrule}
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{50mm}@{\hspace{4mm}}L{\dimexpr\linewidth-54mm\relax}@{}}
  \textcolor{hscbigidea}{\faLightbulb}\enspace\textbf{The big idea} (violet)
    & The main point of a topic in one to three sentences. Read it first.\\\hline
  \textcolor{hsckw}{\faKey}\enspace\textbf{Key words} (teal)
    & The most important terms, each with a one-line meaning. A new word is also printed in
      \term{bold} the first time it appears.\\\hline
  \textcolor{hscformula}{\faIcon{square-root-alt}}\enspace\textbf{Formula card} (blue)
    & The formula (as on the official sheet), each symbol with its SI unit, when to use it,
      where it comes from (if it is not on the sheet) and a common trap.\\\hline
  \textcolor{hscexp}{\faFlask}\enspace\textbf{Key experiment} (brown)
    & Aim, what was done, the result, the conclusion and why it matters.\\\hline
  \textcolor{hscbrand}{\faClipboardList}\enspace\textbf{Summary} (navy)
    & ``In this chapter'' at the start of a chapter; ``Remember'' at the end: the facts to
      know by heart.\\\hline
\end{tabular}}

\smallskip
\noindent The badge under a formula tells you about the official HSC formula sheet:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \hscbadgeonsheet\ \ it is printed on the sheet, so you are given it in the exam;
  \item \hscbadgenotonsheet\ \ learn it, or learn how to derive it;
  \item \hscbadgecombine{two formulas}\ \ join them.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{How to study with these Essentials}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Read \textbf{one chapter a day}: the big idea and the key words first, then the
        facts, the diagrams and the formula cards. Practise each chapter with the full guide.
  \item Use the tables at the back to see the whole module on a few pages: \emph{All
        formulas at a glance}, \emph{Key experiments at a glance} and the \emph{Glossary}
        (with Chinese).
  \item \textbf{The night before the exam} (the exam is on Thursday 5 November 2026),
        re-read the ``Remember'' box at the end of each chapter.
\end{enumerate}
